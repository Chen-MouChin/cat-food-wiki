# AAFP：Feline Feeding Programs（2018）

- 來源：美國貓科獸醫師協會（AAFP）共識聲明；見 data/sources.json
- 類型：獸醫組織共識，7 頁；ingest 範圍：p.1–4（正文與重點）
- ingest：2026-11-19

## 摘要

不只要管「餵什麼」，也要管「怎麼餵」。貓天生少量多餐、單獨進食，一天一兩大餐放在同一處，容易造成肥胖和壓力。建議分成多次小餐、用益智餵食器、多貓家庭分開餵、用秤量份量。

## 主張

- [C-BEH-02] 貓喜歡經常吃、而且單獨吃 ｜ 「Cats are solitary predators that consume small prey, and they prefer to eat often and alone」 p.1
- [C-TRT-01] 零食永遠不要超過總飲食的 10%，而且零食的熱量要算進每天的份量 ｜ 「Treats should never amount to more than 10% of the total diet, and treat calories must be included when calculating daily food allowances」 p.2
- [C-FEED-02] 益智餵食器和把飼料藏在家裡各處，可以增加活動、提供身心刺激 ｜ 「Using puzzle feeders and hiding kibbles around the home increases activity, provides mental and physical stimulation」 p.2
- [C-FEED-09] 益智餵食器有簡單有複雜，有固定的也有會滾動的，乾糧和濕食都有；有些要貓推動餵食器，或用爪子、舌頭才拿得到食物 ｜ 「Puzzle feeders vary in their complexity, can be stationary or rolling, and can be designed for dry or wet foods. Some types require more effort than others on the part of the cat to obtain food, either by manipulation of the feeder or by the use of paws or tongue to reach the food」 p.3
- [C-FEED-01] 每天的份量要分成多次小餐，分散在一整天 24 小時裡餵，可以的話用益智餵食器 ｜ 「The cat’s daily food allowance should be split into multiple small meals and fed throughout the 24 h period, using puzzle feeders when possible」 p.3
- [C-FEED-05] 份量可以用數位電子秤（公克）量 ｜ 「help determine the best way to measure food portions, either by using a digital gram kitchen scale」 p.3
- [C-KCAL-06] 大多數家貓結紮又不太活動，熱量可以先從每公斤體重每天 40–66 大卡開始算 ｜ 「Most house cats are neutered and minimally active. Therefore, use 40–66 kcal/kg body weight/day as a starting point for determining calorie intake」 p.3
- [C-BCS-02] 真正需要的熱量，是能讓貓維持理想體態的量（9 分制的 4–5 分） ｜ 「The true caloric requirement is the amount needed to maintain a cat in optimal body condition (2.5–3/5 or 4–5/9 on a body condition scoring [BCS] scale)」 p.3
- [C-BCS-06] 因此，獸醫和飼主要經常看體態，再適當調整餵的量 ｜ 「Therefore, veterinarians and clients need to monitor body condition frequently and adjust the amount fed appropriately」 p.3
- [C-FEED-03] 多貓家庭要有好幾個彼此看不到的餵食點 ｜ 「Feeding plans should include multiple feeding stations that are visually separated」 p.4
- [C-FEED-04] 餵食點不要靠近砂盆 ｜ 「Additionally, feeding stations should not be close to litter boxes」 p.4

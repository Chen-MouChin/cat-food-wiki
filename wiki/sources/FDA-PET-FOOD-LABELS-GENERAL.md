# FDA：Pet Food Labels - General

- 來源：美國食品藥物管理局（FDA），網頁存檔 2019-04-23；見 data/sources.json
- 類型：政府衛教網頁；ingest 範圍：全文（網頁來源一律記為 p.1）
- ingest：2026-11-19

## 摘要

美國寵物食品標籤由聯邦（FDA）和各州（多採用 AAFCO 的模型規範）兩層管理。這頁逐項解釋標籤：產品名稱的百分比規則、成分表排序、保證分析值怎麼換成乾物比較、營養充足聲明的兩種證明方式與生命階段。

## 主張

- [C-LBL-01] 產品名稱只寫一種食材（例如「鮪魚貓糧」）時，扣掉加工用水，這個食材至少要佔 95% ｜ 「at least 95% of the product must be the named ingredient (beef or tuna, respectively), not counting the water added for processing」 p.1
- [C-LBL-02] 名稱加上「Dinner」「Formula」這類字時，點名的食材只要佔 25% 以上（不到 95%） ｜ 「If the named ingredients comprise at least 25% of the product (not counting the water for processing), but less than 95%, the name must include a qualifying descriptive term, such as “Dinner” as in "Beef Dinner for Dogs." Counting the added water, the named ingredients still must comprise 10% of the product. Many descriptors other than "dinner" are used, however, with "Platter," "Entree," "Nuggets" and "Formula" being a few examples」 p.1
- [C-LBL-03] 「Tuna Cat Food」至少要含 95% 鮪魚，「Cat Food With Tuna」只要 3% ｜ 「whereas the latter example must contain at least 95% tuna, the first needs only 3%」 p.1
- [C-LBL-04] 成分表照重量排序，而且是加進去時含水的重量 ｜ 「The weights of ingredients are determined as they are added in the formulation, including their inherent water content」 p.1
- [C-LBL-06] 鮮肉大約 75% 是水；肉粉在加工時去掉了水和脂肪，水分只有大約 10%，剩下的大多是蛋白質和礦物質 ｜ 「meat is very high in moisture (approximately 75% water). On the other hand, water and fat are removed from meat meal, so it is only 10% moisture (what's left is mostly protein and minerals)」 p.1
- [C-LBL-09] FDA 舉例：一款成分表第一項是「肉」、第二項是玉米；它的廠商說對手那款第一項是玉米、第二項才是「肉粉」，暗示對手的動物性蛋白質比較少 ｜ 「For example, one pet food may list "meat" as its first ingredient, and "corn" as the second ingredient. The manufacturer doesn't hesitate to point out that its competitor lists "corn" first ("meat meal" is second), suggesting the competitor's product has less animal-source protein than its own」 p.1
- [C-LBL-07] 在這個例子裡，把兩款都換算成乾物比較，可以看出對手那款（第二個產品）來自肉粉的動物性蛋白質，比第一款來自鮮肉的還多，雖然成分表的排序看起來相反 ｜ 「If we could compare both products on a dry matter basis (mathematically "remove" the water from both ingredients), one could see that the second product had more animal-source protein from meat meal than the first product had from meat, even though the ingredient list suggests otherwise」 p.1
- [C-LBL-08] 這不代表第二個產品的「肉」比較多，甚至不代表它含有肉；肉粉本身不算是肉，因為大部分脂肪和水已經在提煉時去掉 ｜ 「That is not to say that the second product has more "meat" than the first, or in fact, any meat at all. Meat meal is not meat per se, since most of the fat and water have been removed by rendering」 p.1
- [C-DM-01] 乾物百分比＝100% 減去標籤上的水分百分比 ｜ 「The percentage of dry matter of the product is equal to 100% minus the percentage of moisture guaranteed on the label」 p.1
- [C-DM-02] 換成乾物基礎：保證值 ÷ 乾物百分比 × 100 ｜ 「the percent guarantee should be divided by the percentage of the dry matter, then multiplied by 100」 p.1
- [C-DM-07] 罐頭和乾糧要比較營養含量，必須換成同樣的水分基礎 ｜ 「To make meaningful comparisons of nutrient levels between a canned and dry product, they should be expressed on the same moisture basis」 p.1
- [C-DM-03] 例子：罐頭標 8% 蛋白質、75% 水分（乾物 25%），乾糧標 27% 蛋白質、10% 水分（乾物 90%） ｜ 「a canned food guarantees 8% crude protein and 75% moisture (or 25% dry matter), while a dry food contains 27% crude protein and 10% moisture (or 90% dry matter)」 p.1
- [C-DM-06] 換成乾物後，罐頭的蛋白質是 32%，乾糧是 30%，罐頭反而比較高 ｜ 「the canned contains 32% crude protein on a dry matter basis (8/25 X 100 = 32), while the dry has only 30% on a dry matter basis (27/90 X 100 = 30)」 p.1
- [C-DM-04] 乾糧大約 88–90% 是乾物，罐頭只有大約 22–25% ｜ 「A dry food is approximately 88-90% dry matter, while a canned food is only about 22-25% dry matter」 p.1
- [C-ADQ-01] 標「完整均衡」的飼料，必須用兩種方法之一證明營養充足 ｜ 「pet food must be substantiated for nutritional adequacy by one of two means」 p.1
- [C-ADQ-08] 方法一：配方符合既定的營養標準 ｜ 「The first method is for the pet food to contain ingredients formulated to provide levels of nutrients that meet an established profile」 p.1
- [C-ADQ-09] 方法二：通過 AAFCO 的餵食試驗流程 ｜ 「The alternative means of substantiating nutritional adequacy is for the product to be tested using the appropriate AAFCO Feeding Trial Protocol(s)」 p.1
- [C-ADQ-02] 標「所有生命階段」的飼料，符合的是生長和繁殖這種比較嚴格的標準 ｜ 「meets the more stringent nutritional needs for growth and reproduction」 p.1
- [C-ADQ-03] 「熟齡（senior）」沒有專門的規範，熟齡飼料只要符合成貓維持的標準就好 ｜ 「a "senior" diet must meet the requirements for adult maintenance, but no more」 p.1
